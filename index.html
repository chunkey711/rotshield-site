Rotshield

Screen time control for iPhone

Rotshield closes the apps you choose — on a schedule, all day, or once a daily limit runs out. A focus timer does the same for a single session. To get back in early, you take a short breathing challenge that opens a 15-minute window.

It is a tool for limiting yourself, not other people. There is no account, no server and no analytics: everything Rotshield knows stays on your phone.
