## What Rotshield is

Rotshield is an app for iPhone (iOS 18 or later) that uses Apple's Screen Time framework to close the apps you choose: on a schedule, all day, once a daily limit runs out, or for a focus session. To get back in early you complete a short challenge, which opens a 15-minute window.

It is a self-control tool: you limit your own device. It is not a parental control, it is not a medical or therapeutic product, and there is no account — your rules and history live on your phone.

The shield belongs to iOS. Rotshield asks the system to close an app, and the system does it — so withdrawing Screen Time access or deleting the app undoes the block. Treat it as friction, not a lock.
