## Purchases on this website

Orders on www.rotshield.app are processed by Paddle.com, our online reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle takes the payment, and Paddle issues the refund.

- **14 days from the first payment.** You can ask for a refund of the first payment for a new subscription within 14 days of the date it was charged. You do not have to give a reason.
- **Free trial.** If your plan started with a free trial, you are not charged during it, so there is nothing to refund. The first payment is the one taken when the trial ends, and the 14 days start on that day.
- **Renewals.** Renewal payments are not refundable, except where the law where you live requires it. Cancel before the next renewal to avoid being charged — see [Pricing](/pricing/).
- **After a refund.** Rotshield Pro for the refunded period ends, and the subscription is cancelled, so you will not be charged again.
- **Where the money goes.** Paddle returns it to the payment method you used. How long it takes to show up depends on your bank.

## How to ask for a refund

1. Go to [paddle.net](https://paddle.net) and find your order using the email address you used at checkout — or use the link in your Paddle receipt email — and request a refund.
2. Or write to me at ruslan.d@scalemate.co with that email address, and I will have Paddle issue the refund.

Paddle's own rules for refunds are in the [Paddle Refund Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy) and the [Paddle Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms).

## Cancelling is not the same as a refund

Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not return money already paid. How to cancel is on the [Pricing](/pricing/) page. If you want both, ask for the refund — it cancels the subscription too.

## Purchases in the App Store

If you subscribed inside the app, Apple charged you, and only Apple can refund it — I cannot. Request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), or see Apple's guide [Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223).

## Your legal rights

This policy does not limit your rights as a consumer under the law where you live. In the EU, EEA and UK you have a 14-day right to withdraw from an online purchase; the 14-day refund of your first payment above covers it.
