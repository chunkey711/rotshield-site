## What Rotshield Pro includes

- **Blocking rules** — close the apps and categories you pick in Apple's own picker: on a schedule, all day, or once a daily limit runs out.
- **Focus timer** — block your apps for one stretch of work. In hard mode the session cannot be ended early.
- **The way back in** — a short challenge (a minute of breathing, or naming why you want to open the app) opens a 15-minute window. When it closes, iOS puts the shield back on its own.
- **Today screen** — your Screen Time report, a daily goal, and the brain score the mascot wears all day.
- **Check-ins** — the reasons and moods you logged on the way back in, kept on your phone.

Rotshield runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Your Screen Time data never leaves the phone — see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## How billing works

- **Free trial.** A plan may come with a free trial. If it does, its length is shown before you subscribe. You are not charged during the trial; cancel before it ends and you pay nothing. Otherwise the plan's price is charged when the trial ends. Without a trial, the first payment is taken on the day you subscribe.
- **Automatic renewal.** Both plans renew automatically at the end of each period — every year or every week — at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel.
- **Taxes and currency.** Prices on this page are in US dollars. Depending on where you live, VAT or sales tax may be added at checkout; the total is shown before you pay. Your bank may charge its own fee for paying in a foreign currency.
- **Cancel any time.** Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you are not charged again.

## Where you can subscribe

**In the app, through the App Store.** Apple charges you, in your local currency, at the price the app shows before you confirm. You manage and cancel the subscription in **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions** on your iPhone, at least 24 hours before it renews. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple.

**On this website, through Paddle.** Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders: Paddle charges you, issues the receipt, and handles billing, cancellations and refunds under the [Paddle Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms). After checkout you get an activation link — open it on the iPhone where Rotshield is installed, and Pro unlocks in the app. To cancel, use the link in your Paddle receipt email or go to [paddle.net](https://paddle.net).

A subscription is managed where you bought it: one bought on this website cannot be cancelled in iPhone Settings, and one bought in the App Store cannot be cancelled at paddle.net.
